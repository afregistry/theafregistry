To a Hammer, Everything isn't Always a Nail

08/12/2026

Hammers made us who we are, there are many kinds of hammers, they shaped the world since before history.

A leather working hammer created by Kevin Lee.

The saying goes, to a hammer, everything is a nail. Unfortunately this does a massive disservice to the breadth of things that hammers have been made to hit. The list can go on.

I hope to make a museum to include all these important contributions of the rich history of hammers.
